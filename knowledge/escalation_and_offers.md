# Escalation and Offer Authority

## Standard offers
Relationship managers may approve without escalation a fee waiver for up to 6 months, a free financial health review, a cashback card upgrade, or a savings bonus of up to 1 percent for 12 months.

## Escalated offers
Rate-match offers on deposits, waivers longer than 6 months and any offer to a customer with a balance above 150,000 must be approved by the premium team lead. Escalations are answered within 2 working days.

## Complaints
If a flagged customer has an open complaint, resolve the complaint first. A retention offer made while a complaint is open is often seen as an attempt to buy silence.

## Contact limits
Do not contact the same customer with a retention offer more than once in 90 days. Respect marketing opt-outs at all times; a service call about an account review is allowed, a promotional offer is not.

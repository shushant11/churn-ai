# Retention Playbook

## Purpose
This playbook tells the relationship team what to do once the churn model flags a customer. The model gives a calibrated churn probability and the top drivers. The action depends on the risk band and on which drivers push the risk up. Actions must never be chosen because of a customer's gender or nationality.

## Risk bands
High risk means the probability is at or above the campaign threshold and at least 0.5. Medium risk means the probability is between 60 percent of the threshold and the threshold. Low risk means everything below that. High-risk customers are called by a relationship manager within 5 working days. Medium-risk customers receive a personalised email and an in-app message. Low-risk customers receive no special treatment.

## Inactive members
If IsActiveMember is a top driver, the first step is re-engagement rather than a discount. Offer a free financial health review, help the customer set up the mobile app, and switch on salary credit or auto-pay. Inactive customers who are re-engaged within 30 days are much less likely to leave.

## Three or more products
Customers holding three or four products churn at very high rates in our data. This usually signals product fatigue, overlapping fees or a bad cross-sell. Run a product rationalisation call: review every product, close or merge the ones the customer does not use, and waive the overlapping fees for 6 months. Do not cross-sell further products to this group.

## Single product customers
Customers with exactly one product and a low balance are early in the relationship. Offer one well-matched second product only, such as a fee-free credit card or a savings pocket. Two-product customers are the most loyal group.

## Older customers
When Age is the main driver, customers are often approaching retirement and consolidating accounts. Offer a retirement planning session, pension transfer support and a senior relationship manager. Avoid generic promotional offers.

## High balance customers
When Balance is a top driver and the balance is high, the customer is valuable and may be moving money to a competitor offering a better rate. Escalate to the premium team, review the deposit rate, and consider a rate-match offer within the pricing authority limits.

## Offer budget
Each retention offer costs about 50 units in the model's business assumptions. An offer is only worth making when the expected value saved is larger than its cost, which is why the campaign threshold is chosen by expected profit and not by accuracy.

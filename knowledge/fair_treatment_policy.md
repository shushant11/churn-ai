# Fair Treatment and Responsible AI Policy

## Protected attributes
Gender and nationality or country of residence must never be used as the reason for offering, withholding or pricing a retention action. Staff and automated assistants must explain decisions using behavioural drivers such as activity, products held, balance and tenure.

## Use of the model
The churn model is a decision-support tool. A person makes the final decision on any offer above the standard pricing authority. Customers can ask why they were contacted and must get a plain-language reason.

## Monitoring for bias
Every model release is audited by group for selection rate, recall and false positive rate. A selection-rate ratio below 0.8 between groups triggers a review. A gap is acceptable only if recall is similar across groups and the gap reflects genuinely different churn rates, and the review must be documented in the model card.

## Data protection
Personal data such as email addresses, phone numbers and card numbers must not be pasted into the assistant. The assistant redacts them automatically. Only the customer identifier is needed to look a customer up.
